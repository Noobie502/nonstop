# Data licence

The files in `public/data/` (except `world.json`) are a database built from
the sources below. Because two of them are licensed under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/),
which is "share-alike", this database is made available under the **ODbL 1.0** as well.
You may copy and reuse it under that licence's terms, crediting the sources below.

| Source | What it contributes | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [adsb.lol](https://www.adsb.lol) ADS-B history | Flights seen, aircraft types, takeoff times | ODbL 1.0 |
| [OpenFlights](https://openflights.org/data) | Airline call-sign codes, 2014 aircraft records | ODbL 1.0 |
| [Jonty/airline-route-data](https://github.com/Jonty/airline-route-data) | Routes, operating airlines, scheduled flight times (compiled from FlightsFrom.com) | No licence stated by the publisher |
| [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) (`world.json`) | Country shapes for the map | Public domain |

The weekly build that produces these files (`scripts/`) is not part of the
database and is covered by the copyright notice in `LICENSE`.

This summary is not legal advice.
